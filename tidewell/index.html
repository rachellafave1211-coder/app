FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I keep my cleaner?

Absolutely. Your cleaner stays your cleaner. We take over the scheduling, turnover checklists and back-and-forth, so every guest arrives to a ready home.

Are you local to my area?

We're fully remote, which lets us work with beach rentals anywhere in the country. For anything hands-on, like photos, repairs or restocking, we line up and manage your local vendors for you.

Do I keep control of my listing?

Always. You stay the listing owner on Airbnb, and we're added as your co-host. You can see everything we do, adjust our access or remove us at any time.

How does payment work?

It's automatic. Airbnb's co-host payout splits our share from each booking, so there are no invoices or monthly bills.
