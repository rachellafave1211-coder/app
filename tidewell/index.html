FAQ

Questions owners ask

Do I keep my cleaner?

Yes. You keep the cleaner you already know and trust. We handle the scheduling, turnover checklists and communication so you don't have to.

Are you local?

Tidewell is fully remote, which is how we can serve beach rentals nationwide. In-person tasks like photos, repairs and restocking are handled by your local vendors, and we coordinate all of it for you.

Do I keep control of my listing?

Yes. You remain the owner on Airbnb. We're added as a co-host, so you can see everything, change permissions or remove us at any time.

How do I pay?

Through Airbnb's built-in co-host payout. Our share is split automatically from each booking, so there are no invoices to chase.
