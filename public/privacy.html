Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Payday is a personal budgeting app that plans your spending around your paychecks. This policy explains what information Payday collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Payday is built and run by an independent developer, referred to here as "we".

The short version

  • Payday uses your information only to run your budget. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • Connecting a bank is optional. It happens through Plaid, and Payday never sees your bank username or password.
  • You can disconnect a bank, erase your budget, or ask us to delete your account at any time.

Information Payday collects

  • What you enter: your paychecks, bills, spending categories, expenses, account balances, reminders, tasks and display settings.
  • Your email address, if you sign in to sync across devices. Sign-in uses a one-time link, so there's no password.
  • Bank information, only if you connect a bank: through Plaid, Payday receives the names, types and balances of your accounts and your recent transactions. Payday also stores the bank's name and an access token that lets it fetch new transactions.
  • Files you import (CSV or a Google Sheet you link) are read on your device to create expenses. The file itself isn't uploaded.

How it's used

Only to provide Payday's features: building your paycheck budgets, showing your balances, suggesting categories for imported spending, checking off bills that you've paid, and sending the reminders you turn on. Transactions from your bank are shown to you for review, and only the ones you choose to import are saved to your budget. Balances from your bank are only used if you tap "Use these as my balances".

Where it's stored

  • On your device: your budget is saved in your browser or the installed app.
  • In your account, if you sign in: your budget is stored with Supabase, our database provider, so it can sync across your devices. Database rules make it readable only by your account.
  • Bank access tokens are stored only on our server, in a table the app itself can't read, and are never sent to your device.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run Payday, and only so they can provide their service:

  • Plaid connects your bank. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase stores account data and sends sign-in emails.
  • Vercel hosts the Payday website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell your information.

Your consent and choices

  • Payday only connects to a bank when you choose to, and Plaid asks for your permission before sharing any bank data.
  • Disconnect a bank in Settings → Connect your bank. Payday deletes the access token and tells Plaid to end the connection.
  • Erase your budget in Settings → Your data → Clear all data. While you're signed in, this erases it from your account and all your devices.
  • Delete your account, or ask for a copy of your data, by emailing us (below). We'll respond within 30 days.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you use Payday. Bank access tokens are deleted as soon as you disconnect a bank. When you delete your account, we delete your budget, your bank connections and your sign-in details within 30 days. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time before they're overwritten. We review this policy at least once a year.

Security

All connections to Payday are encrypted with TLS (HTTPS). Data stored with Supabase is encrypted at rest. Each account can only read its own data, and the developer accounts that run Payday are protected with two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected users of any breach as required by law.

Children

Payday isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
